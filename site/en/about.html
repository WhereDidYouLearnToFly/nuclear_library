ABOUT ISOTOPEDIA

ISOTOPEDIA is an educational terminal dedicated to nuclear physics — from the fundamental structure of the atom to the history of the nuclear age.

TOOLS

Interactive visualisations built for exploration:

  • Nuclide Table — chart of all known nuclides with decay modes and half-lives
  • Power Plant Map — worldwide nuclear reactors by type, capacity and status
  • Detonations Map — every major nuclear test since Trinity, 1945

CONTENT

Articles covering radioactivity, fission, fusion, nuclear history and policy.
